# Progress Milestone Map

The Progress Milestone Map is meant to help both students and mentors track development over time, understand where to focus conversations at different stages of the relationship, and stay aligned with each other to improve communication.

#### Students

Use this map to guide you on what to work on in each stage of your mentor relationship. You can also use the tracker to keep track of your progress and help to set up your conversations with your mentor.

#### Mentors

Use this map as a skeleton of what conversations to expect with your mentee. Set expectations with your mentee and identify any gaps you see in their professional development based on their tracked progress.
