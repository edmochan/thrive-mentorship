# Internship and Job Search Timeline and Tracker

Follow the steps below to prepare for and apply to jobs and internships in your field of interest. Use our [Application and Networking Tracker](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/32165379/THRIVE.Job.and.Networking.Tracker.xlsx)
 to keep track of your progress and maintain a running log of your professional contacts.

## Step 1. Exploration and Preparation (9-12 months before)

#### Focus on: Understanding which opportunities are available

- Talk to your mentor and/or advisor about career paths and what skills you need for those careers.
- Consider which elective courses you may want to take in the future to help you build those skills.
- Look up entry-level positions locally on Indeed, Handshake, or other job search sites to determine which skills and software are most commonly required for the careers you want.
- Review the resources contained in this toolkit to prepare yourself for the job search.

## Step 2: Resume-Ready Skill Building (6-9 months before)

#### Focus on: Preparing your resume for future applications

- Work on projects to add to your resume. Projects can be class projects or personal projects. **Projects often matter as much as coursework in early-career hiring decisions.**
- Create a LinkedIn profile and start to build your professional network.
- Create a GitHub portfolio and post your projects to it, with a README for each.
- Talk with your mentor and/or advisor about other ways you can start to create evidence of your skills.
- Create a resume and cover letter that you can use for applications. Follow the guidelines in this toolkit and have at least one person review your drafts and give you constructive feedback.

## Step 3: Applications and Networking (3-6 months before)

#### Focus on: Applying early and building connections

- Apply to internships and jobs as soon as they open. It is not uncommon in tech for jobs to be posted up to a year before they start! Search for positions early and often!
- Submit multiple applications to a variety of jobs. Remember: your resume and experience do not have to be a perfect fit for you to be a strong candidate! Apply to the jobs that interest you and let the hiring manager decide if you are a good fit for the role.
- Grow your network on LinkedIn and follow potential job postings there.
- Attend job fairs or other career-related events on campus.
- Track your applications with the Job Search Tracker.

## Step 4: Interviews and Follow-Ups (1-3 months before)

#### Focus on: Preparing for and completing interviews

- Practice for technical and behavioral interviews. Work with the Office of Career Development and/or your mentor for a mock interview and feedback.
- Prepare personal anecdotes in response to common interview questions using the START format.
- Research each company to which you are applying and be prepared to talk about the specifics of each role.
- Check your email regularly and respond to any emails from jobs timely and professionally. Review the Professional Communication guide for email templates as needed.
- Send a thank you message to your interviewer after each interview.
- Continue to track your interviews and jobs with the Job Search Tracker.

## Step 5: Decisions and Reflections (After)

#### Focus on: Learning and improving

- Reflect on what went well in your job search and what did not. If you did not successfully get a job this time, return to Step 1 and revisit your timeline. **Rejection is normal. Even strong candidates get rejected sometimes!**
- Update your resume, portfolio, and LinkedIn.
- Continue to build your skills and update your materials regularly.
- Continue to grow your professional network and stay in touch with your contacts.

## Conclusions

#### Common mistakes in job searching

- Applying just before the deadline
- Applying to too few positions
- Using the same resume and cover letter for every application
- Assuming rejection means you have failed

#### Keys to success

- Start early and work through this timeline regularly
- Apply to many jobs! Set up alerts for jobs you may want and apply to them soon after they are posted
- Customize your resume and cover letter for every position
- Use your mentor and faculty advisor for feedback
- Update your GitHub, LinkedIn, and other materials regularly (semester breaks are a great time!)
- Stay positive and persistent
- Keep a list of every job you apply to help yourself stay organized. Make your own list or use the [THRIVE Job tracker](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/32165353/THRIVE.Job.and.Networking.Tracker.xlsx) to keep the details straight.
