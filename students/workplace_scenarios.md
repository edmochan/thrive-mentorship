# Workplace Scenario Decision Guide

This guide covers several real-world workplace scenarios that are difficult to navigate for early-career professionals, but also critical to professional success. Many workplace expectations are learned through time and experience. This guide is meant to help you practice them before you get there, to make you more comfortable once they arise.

#### How to use this guide

Read each scenario and think carefully and critically about how you would respond. Discuss your instincts with your advisor or mentor to see if they agree with your handling of the situation. Your mentors likely have experienced these scenarios in their own working life. Discuss their own experiences and how they handled them, and whether they would do things differently in retrospect. Then reflect on what your own answers based on their feedback.

#### Decision-Making Framework

As you review each scenario, ask yourself:

1. What is the situation?
2. What are my options?
3. What is the most professional response?
4. What are the possible outcomes?
